# Review fragments

Reusable instructions for selecting how much review a task deserves.

## Independent review

> Require a reviewer who did not perform the implementation to assess the result before final acceptance. The reviewer should inspect the work from first principles and report concrete concerns, missing evidence, or confirmation that the result is satisfactory.

## Two separate reviewers

> Obtain two separate reviews before final acceptance. Keep the reviews independent enough that each reviewer has a real opportunity to notice different problems, then reconcile their findings before considering the work complete.

## Self-review only

> A separate reviewer is not required for this task. Perform a careful self-review, run the relevant checks, repair any issues you find, and continue without waiting for an additional review step.

## Review only before promotion

> Keep experimental work fast and lightly reviewed. Apply the stronger review requirement only when an experimental result is about to become a durable or authoritative part of the project.

## Proportional review

> Match review effort to the consequence and reversibility of the work. Use lightweight checking for disposable or low-impact changes, and deeper independent scrutiny where an unnoticed mistake would create substantial downstream cost.

## Continue while review is pending

> A required review applies to the result being reviewed; it is not automatically a stop condition for the whole run. Continue other safe and unblocked work while review is pending unless the task explicitly requires a full pause.
